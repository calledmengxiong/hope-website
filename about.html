COMPANY PROFILE

FUJIAN HOPE NEW MATERIAL CO., LTD.

FUJIAN HOPE NEW MATERIAL CO., LTD. is a professional supplier of materials and chemical solutions for the artificial leather and coating industries. Our product range includes colorants and pigments, silicone additives, surface finishing agents, PU resin, release paper, fabric backing materials, plasticizers and artificial leather materials.

We focus on stable product quality, reliable supply, practical technical communication and long-term cooperation with global customers. Through continuous product development and market service, we help customers improve material performance, production efficiency and product competitiveness.

Artificial Leather

High-quality artificial leather materials for furniture, automotive, footwear, bags and upholstery applications. Our products offer excellent durability, texture consistency, flexibility and surface appearance.

Fabric

Professional fabric backing materials including microfiber, nonwoven, knitted and woven fabrics for synthetic leather manufacturing, supporting bonding strength, stability and long-term performance.

Silicone Additives

Functional silicone additives designed to improve smoothness, anti-sticking performance, surface feel, leveling and processing efficiency in synthetic leather and coating applications.

Surface Finishing Agent

Advanced surface finishing agents designed to enhance leather touch, gloss, abrasion resistance, anti-scratch performance and overall appearance for premium finishing solutions.

Colorant / Pigment

High-performance colorants and pigments for synthetic leather, textile and industrial coating applications, providing stable dispersion, strong coloring power and reliable shade consistency.

Release Paper

Premium release paper available in matte, glossy, embossed and silicone-coated finishes, designed for stable texture transfer, clean release performance and high-quality surface effects.

Resin

Comprehensive resin solutions including PU resin, water-based resin, acrylic resin and binder resin for synthetic leather production, providing adhesion, flexibility and processing stability.

Plasticizer

Reliable plasticizer products formulated to improve flexibility, softness, processing performance and durability in PVC and synthetic leather applications.
